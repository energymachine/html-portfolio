About the product images

The blade pictures in these concepts are placeholders taken from existing photos. In the final website, product images will be rendered from the configurator using your CAD files. That way every model and handle design shows the new handle exactly, with consistent lighting and a transparent background. Real photos like this one stay for the mood images.
