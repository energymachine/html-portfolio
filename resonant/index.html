What this demo already does

  
    
    
  • Geometry: blade, both half handles and the internal handle cavity, tessellated from Flared.step. Photo vs CAD checked: blade outline within 1 %, handle width at 70 mm 26.8 mm (photo) vs 26.9 mm (CAD).
    • 
    
  • Wood: rosewood faces with the serial engraving, the real ply stack, the end grain with medallion, the padauk with maple veneers and the maple cap. Everything is photographic, with no procedural noise.
    • 
    
  • Colour-calibrated: lighting and reflectance are measured against your photographs, not set by eye. The rendered front face matches your photo in brightness and colour to within about 1 unit (CIELAB L* 57.4 vs 57.8). Untreated wood reflects almost no direct light, like the real blade. Epoxy, enamel and metal keep their gloss.
    • 
    
  • New in this version: the blade builds itself when the page opens, a guided tour with clickable hotspots, a balance point from your CAD shown on the blade and in a gauge, A/B mode that compares two configurations side by side, and Studio / Workshop / Night backdrops (the workshop is your own bench, from your photo).
    • 
    
  • Two handles, instant switch. Handle 2 follows your CAD volume exactly, and its wood is clipped where the photo runs onto the blade.
    • 
    
  • 360° in every direction: you turn the blade itself, so there is no locked pole. The butt view, the X-ray of the cavity and the exploded ply layers (names from the admin; empty thickness hidden) are each one click.
    • 
    
  • Live price and weight (±5 g). Weight is calculated from CAD volume × wood density − cavity, so new designs get correct weights automatically. A compatibility rule is included as an example.
    • 
    
  • Extras: model label (it follows the model name), 8 flags, and face engraving in the serial-number style. Add to cart (WooCommerce payload), share code and image export (studio, white or transparent background).
    • 
  


  
Technology

  
Three.js (MIT) and WebGL with physically based materials, studio image lighting and a live contact shadow. CAD is converted with OpenCascade. Textures are photo-registered and use triplanar projection, so nothing stretches on the rounded handle sides. Delivered as a WordPress plugin with WooCommerce hooks. There are no subscriptions or paid services; everything runs on your hosting.
