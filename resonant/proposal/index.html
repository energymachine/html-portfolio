What the demo does

Every view your customers need

The blade rotates freely in every direction, so nothing locks at the top or bottom and the bottom of the handle is always one click away. Both handles switch instantly.

Also in the demo: a 4-step configurator; live price and weight (±5 g) calculated from CAD volume × wood density − cavity; a sample compatibility rule; untreated or oiled finish; model label; 8 flags; add to cart with a WooCommerce data preview; share code; image export on a studio, white or transparent background.
