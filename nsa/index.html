01 · Concept

One mark: protection, NextGi and access granted.

A shield with your N drawn as one continuous line that ends in a keyhole. Access is granted only at the end of a verified path, so the mark is zero-trust drawn as a symbol.

01

The shield

Modern and slightly squared, so it reads as enterprise security, not consumer antivirus.

02

The N

One unbroken stroke: the encrypted tunnel carrying every session from endpoint to network.

03

The keyhole

The N's last stroke becomes a keyhole: access at the end of a verified path.

04

The grid

100-unit grid, 9.6-unit stroke, so it stays sharp from a 16 px tray icon to a banner.

Colours: Vault Navy #0A1633 carries authority; Signal Teal #14D3C1 is kept for moments of access. Type: Inter (SIL OFL), free for commercial use. A "by NextGi" lockup keeps NSA under the NextGi umbrella.
